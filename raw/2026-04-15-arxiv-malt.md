## Abstract

Multi-agent LLM training improves performance on reasoning tasks by assigning specialized roles (generator, verifier, refinement model) and utilizing joint outcome-based rewards to enhance collaboration among models. The sequential multi-agent setup with heterogeneous LLMs demonstrates trajectory-expansion and credit assignment strategies.

## Key Points

- Sequential multi-agent setup: generator -> verifier -> refinement
- Joint outcome-based rewards for multi-agent credit assignment
- Demonstrated on MATH, GSM8k, CQA benchmarks
- Uses Llama 3.1 8B models in specialized roles
- 46 HuggingFace upvotes, active community discussion
- Builds on cooperative MARL principles applied to LLM reasoning
