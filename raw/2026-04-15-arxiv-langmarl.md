## Abstract

LangMARL addresses how LLM-based agents can better develop coordination strategies in multi-agent environments by applying credit assignment mechanisms from classical MARL to language-based systems. The framework enables agents to extract causal relationships from trajectories for improved feedback quality.

## Key Points

- Agent-level language credit assignment bridging classical MARL with language models
- Gradient evolution in language space for policy improvement
- Dense feedback from replay trajectory causal relations under sparse rewards
- Improved sample efficiency vs baseline LLM-agent approaches
- Enhanced interpretability and generalization across cooperative tasks
