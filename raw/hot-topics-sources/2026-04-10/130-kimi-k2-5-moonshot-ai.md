How to use Kimi K2.5?

Kimi K2.5 is available across Kimi Web, App, API, and Kimi Code. Kimi Web and App offer four modes: Instant for quick responses, Thinking for complex questions, Agent for research and content creation, and Agent Swarm (Beta) for large-scale tasks.

1

Describe your request

Select your mode (Instant, Thinking, Agent, or Agent Swarm) and tell Kimi what you need in natural language. You can type your instructions or upload files, images, and videos as reference.

2

Kimi gets to work

Kimi processes your request in real time. For chat modes, you'll see responses as they're generated. For Agent and Agent Swarm modes, Kimi researches, creates, and organizes content based on your goals.

3

Review your results

Chat modes deliver answers directly in the conversation. Agent modes provide a preview of your outputs (Docs, Slides, Sheets, Websites, Research Reports), so you can review, download, or share when ready.

Questions & Answers

What's the difference between Kimi K2.5 models: Instant, Thinking, Agent, and Agent Swarm?

Instant is best for quick questions and simple responses.

Thinking is suited for problems that require deeper reasoning or step-by-step analysis.

Agent is best for research and content creation tasks that involve generating structured outputs such as documents, slides, spreadsheets, websites, or detailed reports.

Agent Swarm is intended for large-scale or multi-step projects where different parts of the work can run in parallel.

What is agent swarm?

Agent swarm is a multi-agent system where specialized AI agents work together on the same task. Each agent is responsible for a specific role, such as research, coding, or reasoning. This approach enables parallel execution across large, multi-step workflows.

When should I use Kimi K2.5 Agent Swarm?

Use Kimi K2.5 Agent Swarm when a task is too large or complex for a single agent. It is ideal for large-scale research, long-form writing, and batch operations such as downloading or processing many files at once. By coordinating multiple AI agents in parallel, Kimi Agent Swarm accelerates large, multi-step projects and delivers results much faster.

How is Kimi K2.5 different from previous Kimi models?

Kimi K2.5 represents a qualitative shift from earlier Kimi models. Instead of focusing on single-task assistance, Kimi K2.5 is designed for real-world execution. It natively understands text, images, and video, enabling visual-to-code workflows, and introduces agent swarm capabilities for coordinating complex, long-horizon tasks.

As a result, Kimi K2.5 supports complete work outputs such as documents, spreadsheets, slides, websites, and in-depth research tasks, and scales to large, multi-step projects executed in parallel.
